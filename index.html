Scope Boundaries & Technical FAQ

Best suited for constant offset corrections (trimmed intros, slate delays). For progressive drift caused by 23.976fps to 25fps frame-rate conversion, use linear stretch ratio scaling.

What happens if shifting 3 seconds earlier pushes Cue 1 below 00:00:00,000?

SubShift automatically clamps sub-zero start times to 00:00:00,000 while keeping a minimum readable duration, preventing Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve import errors.

Why not just paste my SRT file into ChatGPT to shift timestamps?

LLMs regenerate text token-by-token: files above 150 lines often truncate, base-60 minute rollovers miscalculate, and approved translations get rewritten. SubShift modifies only millisecond integers.

Do exported files import cleanly into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and CapCut?

Yes. Files strictly conform to SubRip (HH:MM:SS,mmm) and WebVTT (HH:MM:SS.mmm) UTF-8 specifications without broken cue headers.
